Can mathematics become an artistic medium?

I’ve been exploring the intersection of physics, quantum information, and creative programming—and recently put that curiosity into a working application.

BYTE / GLITCH is an audio-reactive visualizer that transforms sound into evolving, quantum-inspired geometric artwork. Built with JavaScript, the Web Audio API, and Canvas rendering, it analyzes audio in real time and targets 30 FPS.

The project draws on concepts I studied while earning Coursera certificates in:

• Particle Physics: an Introduction — University of Geneva
• Introduction to Quantum Information — Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)

It’s an artistic interpretation of mathematical concepts—not a simulation of quantum hardware.

Try the interactive version:
https://qubyte-chawk.netlify.app

Play NEGATIVE VELOCITY, an original track from my music project artificialflowers, or upload your own song to see how the geometry responds.
